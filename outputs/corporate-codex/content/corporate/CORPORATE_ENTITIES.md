## EXEX

**Ticker:** EXEX  
**Full Name:** Extraterrestrial Excavation  
**Type:** Off-world mining / resource infrastructure  
**Category:** Corporation  
**Status:** Core canon  
**Base Price Suggestion:** 515.42  
**Volatility:** Medium  
**Trend Bias:** Slightly positive  
**Visual Mood:** powerful, stable, old corporate capital  

### Short Profile

EXEX is possibly the most powerful corporation in the setting. Space mining began with them, and no one has surpassed them yet.

They present themselves as builders of critical extraterrestrial infrastructure, not as military actors. They claim to dig, refine, transport, and stabilize. But on Proxima b, extraction infrastructure has become inseparable from force.

### Role in the Conflict

EXEX is deeply exposed to Proxima Centauri b because of its extraction claims, pipelines, processing nodes, and resource futures.

EXEX grows when pipelines work.

EXEX falls when public scandals become impossible to contain.

### Public Language

- critical infrastructure continuity;
- licensed extraction;
- operational complexity;
- responsible extraterrestrial development;
- commercial confidentiality;
- stakeholder value;
- extraction stability.

### Risk Tags

- Public Visibility
- Legal Exposure
- Pipeline Dependency
- Contractor Exposure
- Proxima Resource Reliance

### Related

- OPSEC
- UNICOL
- PSA
- PXB-X
- OCI

### Example Headlines

- Company denies operational role in Corridor 12-B incident.
- EXEX secures disputed extraction corridor after infrastructure disruption.
- Analysts raise target price on continued Proxima resource flow.
- EXEX cites flare activity and multi-actor environment in response to UNICOL report.

### Excerpt

> EXEX is not a military organization.  
> EXEX does not conduct combat operations.  
> EXEX is an extraction company operating critical infrastructure in one of the most complex environments ever opened to human industry.

### Short Profile

OPSEC is a private military and operational security company created from former soldiers and remnants of a once-existing regular army.

OPSEC does not sell war publicly. It sells continuity, guarantees, and risk reduction.

OPSEC grows when pipelines explode, insecurity rises, and corporations need protection.

## UNICOL

**Ticker:** UNICOL  
**Full Name:** United Colonies  
**Type:** Peacekeeping / political institution  
**Category:** Institution / PvE complication  
**Status:** Core canon  
**Base Price Suggestion:** 42.80  
**Volatility:** Medium-high  
**Trend Bias:** Negative  
**Visual Mood:** bureaucratic, weak, concerned, underfunded  

### Short Profile

UNICOL is a satirical take on helpless peacekeeping institutions.

After the Proxima b conflict escalated, colonial governments introduced a peacekeeping contingent. Due to underfunding, corruption, poor equipment, and weak mandate, UNICOL mostly fields obsolete fragile peacekeeper robots.

They are not a serious military match for corporate operatives or OPSEC assets except by numbers.

### Role in the Conflict

UNICOL marks corridors, issues reports, observes incidents, requests restraint, and sometimes physically gets in the way.

UNICOL is mostly known through reports about another outpost, convoy, observer unit, or headquarters being accidentally destroyed.

### Public Language

- deep concern;
- mandate-limited response;
- deconfliction corridor;
- civilian movement disruption;
- unresolved thermal signatures;
- humanitarian access remains restricted;
- all parties must exercise restraint.

### Risk Tags

- Mandate Limited
- Equipment Obsolete
- Public Scandal
- Observer Casualties
- Corridor Failure

### Related

- PSA
- EXEX
- OPSEC
- OCI

### Example Headlines

- Observer Mission confirms loss of contact with Post 12-B.
- UNICOL calls on all parties to respect civilian movement corridors.
- Peacekeeping units non-responsive after kinetic activity near extraction belt.
- UNICOL report cites unresolved thermal signatures in restricted sector.

### Excerpt

> UNICOL emphasizes that unresolved thermal signatures should not be interpreted as confirmed fatalities until physical access is restored.  
> UNICOL further emphasizes that lack of physical access should not be interpreted as absence of fatalities.

## PSA

**Ticker:** PSA  
**Full Name:** Proxima Settlement Authority  
**Type:** Local administration / weak political authority  
**Category:** Political actor  
**Status:** Core canon  
**Base Price Suggestion:** 18.25  
**Volatility:** High  
**Trend Bias:** Unstable  
**Visual Mood:** weak authority, performative sovereignty  

### Short Profile

PSA is the semi-legitimate local administration on Proxima Centauri b.

It exists as a political actor but has little real control. It issues condemnations, emergency directives, licensing notices, civilian corridor designations, and public statements.

It may not control the ground, but everyone has to acknowledge that it exists.

### Role in the Conflict

PSA is the administrative ghost of a state. It provides legal noise, weak legitimacy, disputed licenses, and public pressure.

It may influence gameplay through:
- legal modifiers;
- mission constraints;
- civilian corridor rules;
- public exposure changes;
- licensing disputes;
- emergency directives.

### Public Language

- temporary suspension;
- recognized civilian corridor;
- administrative review;
- provisional licensing;
- unacceptable escalation;
- public order.

### Risk Tags

- Weak Enforcement
- Political Noise
- License Disputes
- Civilian Corridors
- Low Legitimacy

### Related

- UNICOL
- EXEX
- PXB-X
- OCI

### Example Headlines

- Emergency directive issued for disputed civilian corridor.
- PSA condemns renewed armed activity near settlement clusters.

**Category:** Index  
**Status:** Core market object
