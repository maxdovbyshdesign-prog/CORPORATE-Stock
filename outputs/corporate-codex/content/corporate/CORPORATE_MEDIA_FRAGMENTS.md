## 3. UNICOL Incident Report Excerpt

**Format:** peacekeeping report  
**Use:** UNICOL detail page, media feed, report modal  

UNICOL Observer Mission on Proxima Centauri b confirms that a significant security deterioration occurred in and around Corridor 12-B between local timestamps 03:14 and 05:47 during the current operational cycle.

The incident affected one UNICOL monitoring compound, two civilian transit routes, three registered habitation clusters, and an undetermined number of informal settlement structures located outside currently recognized administrative boundaries.

At this time, UNICOL is unable to independently verify the total number of civilian casualties.

Preliminary field data indicates substantial civilian movement disruption, localized shelter degradation, loss of access to water distribution points, and multiple unresolved thermal signatures detected in the affected area following the conclusion of kinetic activity.

UNICOL calls on all licensed and unlicensed armed actors in the sector to exercise restraint, respect civilian movement corridors, and refrain from actions that may further complicate humanitarian access.

**Format:** analyst note  
**Use:** finance tab  

EXEX rises when pipelines work.

OPSEC rises when pipelines explode.

This rare divergence allows both the extractor and the security provider to benefit from different phases of the same conflict.

If infrastructure disruption increases, OPSEC captures value through increased demand for security, continuity, and deniable response capacity.

The conflict is therefore not a simple market risk. It is a multi-sided revenue environment.
